## Remaining coverage limits

- The in-app run confirms functional states and asset gating, not frame rate or
  memory use on an actual Intel HD 4600-class device.
- It does not automatically drive every weapon's reload/chamber/inspect action,
  validate a physically held right-mouse ADS path, or capture audible playback
  of every voice clip. Those remain separate iterative acceptance targets.
- The dense forest still uses a performance-oriented procedural/card backdrop;
  the six Fir Sapling derivatives are bounded close detail rather than a claim
  of a fully authored high-detail 3D tree pack.
